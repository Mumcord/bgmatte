# Origin

The code of this crate was taken from the `crates/effects` directory of
[Mumcord](https://github.com/Mumcord/mumcord) at commit
`a611a7917c242b7185a492a363a00c45f833a4f3` and continues here with a fresh history under
`MIT OR Apache-2.0`. Mumcord itself is licensed under `AGPL-3.0-only`.

Mumcord's camera capture, its effects thread, its settings and the test camera stayed in
Mumcord, which uses this crate for its background effects.

The bundled models come from Google's MediaPipe and keep their Apache-2.0 licence; see
[NOTICE](NOTICE) and [models/README.md](models/README.md).
